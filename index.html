Common questions

How does sealing work? Every photo is hashed (SHA-256) the moment it's captured. Sealing locks the proof and combines all photo hashes, timestamps and labels into one fingerprint, which is countersigned by an independent timestamp authority. Any change afterwards would break the fingerprint.

I bought the unlock on another device. Open the paywall and tap "Restore purchase" — purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

I deleted a proof by mistake. Deleted proofs cannot be recovered — they exist only on your device and we hold no copies.
