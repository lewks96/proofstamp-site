ProofVault Privacy Policy

Effective 5 July 2026

The short version: ProofVault collects no data. Your photos, reports and personal information never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ProofVault has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. We never see your photos, your location, or anything else about you.

Photos

Photos you capture or import are stored only in ProofVault's local database on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. PDF reports are generated on your device and are only shared when — and with whom — you choose to share them.

Location

ProofVault can optionally stamp your evidence photos with the location they were taken, to strengthen your proof. This is entirely optional — if you decline location access, the app works fully without it. Location data, like everything else, is stored only on your device and appears only in the reports you generate.

Independent timestamps

When you seal a proof (and only then), ProofVault sends the proof's 32-byte cryptographic fingerprint to a public timestamp authority (Sectigo or FreeTSA) over an encrypted connection, to obtain independent evidence of when your proof existed. The fingerprint is an anonymous hash: it contains no personal information, cannot be reversed to reveal your photos, and is sent with no account, identifier, or cookie that links it to you. No other data is transmitted.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details, or any other personal information from a purchase.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Delete a proof inside the app, or delete the app, and it is gone. We could not recover it for you if we wanted to — we never had it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ProofVault ever changes how data is handled (for example, an optional iCloud backup), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the App Store release notes.
